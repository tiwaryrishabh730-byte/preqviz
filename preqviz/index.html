    About

    Know what an elective expects — before you pick it.

    Preqviz maps every Semester 4 elective for Bachelor of Technology, Artificial Intelligence students to the topics it builds on, then checks them against the subjects you have completed.

    How it works

    01

    Prerequisites form a graph

    Every elective and topic is a node; an arrow means “needs”. A topic shared by several electives is stored once.

    02

    A C program explores it

    One Depth-First Search per elective finds every prerequisite, gives the study order, and rejects data with a cycle.

    03

    This page draws the results

    The page has no graph logic of its own. It shows what the C program computed, and checks it against your subjects.

    What the labels mean

    CoveredA subject you have completed teaches it. ReviseYou rated the subject weak, or met the topic only briefly. New to youIt comes from a subject you have not studied. EssentialThe elective cannot be followed without it. HelpfulIt speeds things up but can be picked up along the way. PartialAn open elective mapped from its short catalogue description.

    Where the data comes from

    • Program electives: the official syllabi for Academic Year 2026–27.
    • Open electives: the even-semester catalogue. Detailed syllabi are released after selection, so these are estimates.
    • Maps have not yet been reviewed by faculty. Use them as a guide, not a rule.

    Your data

    No account. The subjects you tick are saved only in this browser.
